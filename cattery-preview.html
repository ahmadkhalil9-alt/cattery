About

Small on purpose.

Wrenhollow started with one queen and no plan. Twelve years on we still keep the numbers low, because the thing that makes a good pet cat is not genetics alone — it is having been handled, talked to, and stepped over every day since birth.

Our queens live as house cats and retire here. Kittens are born in the bedroom, move to the kitchen at four weeks, and meet the vacuum cleaner, the doorbell, and at least one visiting child before they go home.

  • Health testing. Both parents screened for HCM and PKD, results available on request.
  • At twelve weeks. Kittens leave fully vaccinated, microchipped, wormed, and litter trained.
  • Afterwards. We answer questions for the life of the cat, and we take our cats back at any age if circumstances change.

Visiting

How it works.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Tell us about your household — other pets, children, whether anyone is home during the day. There are no wrong answers, it just helps us match temperaments.

  2. 02

    Come and meet everyone

    Visits happen once kittens are six weeks old and fully vaccinated adults are around. Expect to be sat on.

  3. 03

    Reserve, then wait

    A deposit holds a kitten. They go home at twelve weeks, not before, with a pedigree, registration papers, and four weeks of insurance.
