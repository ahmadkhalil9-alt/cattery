About us

Cats first, always

Jerusalem Top Cattery is a small home cattery.

We specialise in Scottish cats, both Folds and Straights.

  • Health first. Parents are screened before breeding.
  • Ready to go home. Kittens leave vaccinated and wormed.
  • We stay in touch. Ask us anything for the life of your cat.
